How to Play

Add players, choose a mode, and hit Start Game. Each round, spin the slots — if all 3 match, you hit the Jackpot and get a challenge!

  • 🍺🍺🍺 : Drink up
  • 🍷🍷🍷 : Truth or drink
  • 😂😂😂 : Tell your funniest embarrassing story
  • 🍑🍑🍑 : Sexy question
  • 🍆🍆🍆 : Sexy & spicy question
  • 👽👽👽 : Crazy dare
  • ❤️❤️❤️ : Romantic challenge

Use Too Hot if a challenge is too intense — it will fall back to a local rule instead.
